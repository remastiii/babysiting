Frequently asked questions – Babysitting in Belgrade

How much does a babysitter cost in Belgrade per hour?

Our rates start from 890 RSD/h, depending on the type of service, urgency and duration. Long-term and regular bookings are priced more favourably than short-notice or emergency care. All prices are agreed transparently before the service begins.

Where can I find emergency babysitting in Belgrade?

BabyGuard offers emergency childcare in Belgrade — we can send a verified babysitter to your home within 60 minutes, whether you're in the city centre, Zemun, New Belgrade or the suburbs.

Do you offer babysitters for children with special needs?

Yes! Our team includes caregivers trained to support children with allergies, ADHD, autism and other special needs. All have experience, references and first aid certification.

What are the rates for overnight childcare in Belgrade?

Overnight care (8 PM–7 AM) costs between 8,000 and 12,000 RSD per night, depending on duration and location. The price is always agreed in advance — no hidden fees.

Are your babysitters verified and trustworthy?

Every caregiver undergoes a strict verification process: ID check, references from previous families, personal interview, experience validation and safety training. With over 5 years of experience, your safety is our top priority.
